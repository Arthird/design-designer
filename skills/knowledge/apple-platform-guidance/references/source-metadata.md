## Freshness process

1. Confirm the target Apple platform and framework.
2. Re-check the applicable HIG page for the named control or pattern.
3. Separate platform guidance from technical/accessibility requirements.
4. Update the checked date and relevant decision rules when guidance changes.
5. Re-run the relevant platform-dependent manual workflow.

Do not blend Apple and Material rules into one universal checklist.
