## Freshness process

1. Confirm the project uses Material Design 3 and note its framework/version.
2. Re-check the relevant Material component, layout, or token-role documentation.
3. Update decision rules and checked date when Material guidance changes.
4. Manually re-check adaptive layouts and state behavior on representative targets.

Do not use Material guidance as a blanket substitute for project-specific accessibility or platform requirements.
